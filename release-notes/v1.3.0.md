The media card keeps time, and gets out of the way.

## The progress bar moves

It used to sit still and only jump when the skin was reloaded. Two things were
behind that, and both are fixed.

- **The bar and the elapsed time now advance as the track plays**, rather than
  stepping whenever a reading happened to land. The card is told where the
  track had got to and when it was told, so it carries that forward itself.
- **Skips, seeks and play/pause register as they happen.** Kerf now listens to
  the Windows media session instead of asking it every so often, so a change
  reaches the card at once rather than a beat later.

This also covers the card being behind a window. It used to come back showing
whatever it last saw; now it counts the time in and is simply right.

## It hides when nothing is playing

With no music on, the card fades away, and fades back when something starts.

- **A paused track keeps it up.** A pause is usually a moment long, and the
  card is what tells you where you left off.
- **Skipping a track will not make it flicker.** It waits a couple of seconds
  before going, because a track change reads as stopped in passing.
- **Turn it off with Auto-hide** in the panel's Media tab, and the card stays
  put and says nothing is playing, the way it used to.

## Building from source

The helper now needs one more assembly, the WinRT projection, for the media
session's events. Like the rest, it ships with Windows and .NET Framework, so
there is still no SDK to install.

The README's instructions for testing a working copy no longer copy your
settings over the installed ones -- that reset every layout, size and colour
the panel had written -- and no longer delete fonts the repository does not
carry.

## Install

Download `Kerf-1.3.0.rmskin` below and double-click it. Installing over 1.2.1
keeps your settings.
