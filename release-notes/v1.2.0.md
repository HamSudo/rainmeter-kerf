Kerf now shows what you are listening to.

## The media card

A new module: a compact card for whatever is playing, in one horizontal row --
the cover, then a rule, then the track, the artist, the music line and a
progress bar.

- **It follows everything.** The card reads the Windows media session, the
  same one behind the volume flyout, so a browser tab, Spotify or a local
  player all work. Nothing to install and no player-specific plugin.
- **The music line is the clock's.** The same waveform, pulse or glow, driven
  by what is actually coming out of the speakers.
- **A live stream shows its elapsed time** rather than a progress bar that
  could never fill.

## Square cover or spinning record

The artwork comes two ways, set in the panel.

- **Square** -- the cover as the player hands it over.
- **Disc** -- Kerf cuts the cover into a record, hole and all, and turns it
  while the track plays. It eases up to speed and coasts to a stop when you
  pause.

## Make it yours

- The Customize panel has a **Media** tab: size, transparency, hover,
  alignment and the artwork style.
- Cover, title, artist, pulse and progress bar can each be turned off, and the
  rows close up over whatever is hidden.
- The rule between the cover and the track takes your accent colour.

## Also

- The card is turned on alongside the clock, CPU and GPU the first time Kerf
  runs. Upgrading from 1.1.0? Turn it on from Rainmeter's Manage window, under
  `Kerf\Media`.
- The sensor helper reads the media session too. It uses the WinRT metadata
  that ships with Windows 10 and 11, so there is still nothing extra to
  install and no SDK needed to build it.
- Cover art is written to `%TEMP%` and swept up behind itself.

## Install

Download `Kerf-1.2.0.rmskin` below and double-click it. Installing over 1.1.0
keeps your settings.
