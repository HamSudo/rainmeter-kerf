The ink now keeps up with wallpapers that move.

## Moving wallpapers

Video wallpapers and Wallpaper Engine scenes used to leave the ink behind: it
was read when the wallpaper changed and then only every ten minutes, so text
could sit dark on a dark frame long after the scene moved on.

- **Motion is noticed on its own.** A wallpaper that changes by itself is
  followed every couple of seconds, and a still one costs nothing extra.
- **No flicker.** Readings are smoothed over several seconds and the ink waits
  at least ten seconds between flips, so a passing bright frame can't flip it.
- **Busy backdrops get a steady ink.** When the picture keeps swinging between
  light and dark, Kerf stops chasing it: it picks the ink that reads best
  against the worst frame and strengthens the shadow instead.

## Lighter

- Each reading of the backdrop takes about a third of the CPU it used to. With
  a moving wallpaper the sensor helper uses around 0.4% of one CPU core.
- Nothing is read at all while the desktop is covered, and even checking
  whether it is covered now happens half as often.

## Also

- The Customize panel's footer links to this repository.
- Three optional values under `HKCU\Software\Kerf\Ink` tune the ink:
  `InkLiveMode` (`auto`, `on`, `off`), `InkLiveSeconds` (default 2) and
  `InkRecheckMinutes` (default 10).
- Releases are now built and published by GitHub Actions, and every change is
  checked on a clean machine before it ships.

## Install

Download `Kerf-1.1.0.rmskin` below and double-click it. Installing over 1.0.0
keeps your settings.
