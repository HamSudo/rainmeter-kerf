The music line follows the beat again.

## What was wrong

The glow measured the bass against its own running average to decide when to
pulse -- but that average caught up with the music inside about a second. Play
anything with a steady level and the difference collapsed to nothing, so the
beat stopped registering and the line just sat there glowing faintly in the
middle. Only an occasional peak that stood out from everything around it got
through.

## What it does now

- **It reads the beat, not just the surprises.** The bass is tracked with two
  envelopes -- one quick enough to ride each kick, one slow enough to stay the
  floor it is measured against -- so the line moves with the rhythm through
  loud steady passages instead of going quiet in them.
- **Quiet and loud music both swing the full range.** The loudest beat of the
  last few seconds sets the scale, so a soft track pulses as clearly as a
  loud one rather than barely showing.
- **Loudness and beat stack instead of competing.** How loud the music is sets
  how brightly the line rests, and the beat rides on top of that. It also
  rests brighter than it used to.

This is the same line under the clock and on the media card, so both change
together.
